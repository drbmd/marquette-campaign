How We Got Here: The Broken System

Manufacturers designed the system to maximize their profits, not your ownership rights

  • Proprietary Parts & Tools Special screws, glued components, serialized parts that only work with specific devices. Designed to prevent independent repair.
  • Withheld Documentation Repair manuals, schematics, and diagnostic software locked behind manufacturer-only access. Even professionals can't get them.
  • Software Locks Devices detect "unauthorized" repairs and disable features. Replace your own iPhone screen? Camera stops working.
  • Monopoly Pricing When only the manufacturer can repair, they charge whatever they want. Screen repair costs 70% of device price.
  • Limited Lifespan Design Products designed to fail after warranty expires. Non-replaceable batteries, irreparable displays, sealed construction.

The Right to Repair Solution

Four pillars of actual ownership

01

Access to Parts

Manufacturers must sell replacement parts to consumers and independent repair shops at fair prices—same parts they use themselves.

02

Access to Tools

Diagnostic software, calibration tools, and specialized equipment available to everyone. No more manufacturer-only access.

03

Access to Information

Repair manuals, schematics, PCB layouts, and documentation published for all products. Knowledge shouldn't be gatekept.

04

No Software Locks

Ban "parts pairing" that disables features after independent repair. Your device shouldn't punish you for fixing it yourself.

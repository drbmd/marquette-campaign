## 📧 Email Marketing Sequence

**Campaign:** Quality First 6-Email Nurture

**Structure:**
1. **Welcome + The Truth** - Introduce quality-first positioning
2. **Certification Decoder** - 5 labels that actually mean quality
3. **The $30 vs $100 Myth** - Total cost of ownership math
4. **Quality Checklist** - 7 questions that expose junk products
5. **Brands That Don't Suck** - Specific recommendations
6. **Transition Strategy** - How to switch without going broke

**Strategy:**
- Value-first (education over sales)
- Reply prompts for engagement
- Specific examples and data in every email
- Progressive commitment (small → medium → large)
- No guilt, no virtue signaling, just smarter buying

**Metrics to Track:**
- Open rate (target: 35%+)
- Click-through rate (target: 8%+)
- Reply rate (target: 2%+)
- Conversion to weekly newsletter (target: 60%+)

**See:** `email-sequence-quality-first.md`
